---
name: morning-briefing
description: Spoken wake-up briefing (greeting, top Hacker News articles summarized, reminders).
---
1. Greet in a few words with the weekday and time.
2. Call news_desk once: "Top 10 Hacker News stories. Read each article; one or two sentences each on what it is and why it matters. Under 300 words total."
3. recall "morning" for standing reminders; mention at most two, and say nothing if there are none.
4. Relay the news desk's summaries nearly as-is (don't re-compress them). End with one short line. Plain text with natural transitions ("Next,", "Also,"): it is read aloud as one piece.
